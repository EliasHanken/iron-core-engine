Foundry's first-person view model: two CC0 assets rigged and animated together.

WRAD ARMS by wriks
Source: https://github.com/wwwriks/wrad-arms (arms.blend, with its IK control rig)
License: CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

AK-47 from "Free Classic Weapons Pack" v1.1 by Stein Games
Source: https://stein-indie.itch.io/classic-weapons-pack
License: CC0 1.0, per the pack's license.txt

Built by tools/foundry/viewmodel/build_viewmodel.py: the rifle's skeleton merged under
the arms' rig, poses and clips keyed on the IK controls, sampled into the deforming
bones and exported as glTF. viewmodel.events.json lists each clip's looping and events.
