Foundry's first-person view models (ak47, m1911, m700): CC0 assets rigged and
animated together.

WRAD ARMS by wriks
Source: https://github.com/wwwriks/wrad-arms (arms.blend, with its IK control rig)
License: CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

AK-47, M1911 and M700 from "Free Classic Weapons Pack" v1.1 by Stein Games
Source: https://stein-indie.itch.io/classic-weapons-pack
License: CC0 1.0, per the pack's license.txt

The M700's scope is modelled by tools/foundry/viewmodel/scope.py (no third-party asset).

Built by tools/foundry/viewmodel/build_viewmodel.py: each gun's skeleton merged under
the arms' rig, poses and clips keyed on the IK controls, the hands fitted onto the
gun's surface, sampled into the deforming bones and exported as glTF. Each
<gun>.events.json lists that model's clips, their looping and their events.
